# Noahic covenant commentaries — reading tracker

Reading Gen. 6:18, 8:20-22, and 9:1-17 across the "BEST" Genesis commentaries at [reformedbooksonline.com](https://reformedbooksonline.com/commentaries/old-testament-commentaries/commentaries-on-genesis/#best), checked specifically against Zachary Garris's "Seven Arguments against the Klinean/R2K Division of the Noahic Covenant" (`research/reading-notes/contemporary-kline-critics/garris-critique-of-modern-r2k.md`) and the natural-law section of that same chapter. Occasioned by the project owner's pastor teaching a session on the Noahic covenant from a Klinean perspective — see `research/noahic-covenant-cheat-sheet.md` for the synthesized prep document this reading fed into.

## Done

- **Murphy** (1863) — `murphy-genesis-6-9.md`. Covenant unity ("Gen. 9 formally confirms Gen. 6:18"); sacrifice read as expiatory/substitutionary; civil government grounded richly in the image of God.
- **Leupold** (1942) — `leupold-genesis-6-9.md`. Covenant unity, but via a *different and complicating* philological route than Murphy (heqim reads as prospective at 6:18, not confirmatory); sacrifice read as thanksgiving *and* propitiation blended; civil government reading matches Murphy's.
- **Poole, *Synopsis Criticorum*** (1669, Latin, WELL-ATTESTED not CONFIRMED — own unverified translation) — `poole-synopsis-genesis-6-9.md`. Catalogs multiple readings of 6:18 including one tying it directly to the Gen. 3:15 promised-seed covenant; 9:9 gloss ("ratify a covenant already entered into") matches Murphy against Leupold; sacrifice read as gratitude/worship-acceptance, no expiation language found.
- **Calvin** (1554) — `calvin-genesis-6-9.md`. Strongest covenant-unity case yet (uses Gen. 9's structure to refute Anabaptists on infant covenant-inclusion); but explicitly *denies* the sacrifice had any expiatory/meritorious efficacy in itself — a real complication, not a confirmation, for the expiation argument.

## Bookmarked, not yet read — backup tier

Lower priority per the original triage (see conversation 2026-08-06): worth a look only if a specific gap in the above four still needs filling, or if a commentator holding the *opposite* (divided-covenant) reading is ever needed for balance, since all four read so far converge on unity.

- **Candlish**, *The Book of Genesis Expounded in a Series of Discourses* (1868) — Free Church of Scotland, rich expository/theological register.
- **Jacobus**, *Notes Critical & Explanatory on the Book of Genesis* (1866) — Princeton-trained, critical/philological notes.
- **Lange (ed.)**, *Commentary on the Genesis* (1866) — German critical-conservative series, likely has historical-critical (JEDP-adjacent) engagement with the two-covenant-mentions question specifically.
- **Alford**, *The Book of Genesis & Part of the Book of Exodus* (1872) — Anglican, philological/textual register.

Skipped entirely (devotional/homiletical register, low odds of engaging the technical covenant-unity question): De Graaf, W.H. Griffith Thomas, Johannes Vos, Luther, Edersheim, Fuller.
