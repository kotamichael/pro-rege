# Patrick Gillespie's "Ark" works — synced with Berith Press transcription

Not part of the four-strand natural-law reading project (`research/natural-law-synthesis-reading-map.md`) — this is core covenant-of-redemption/covenant-of-grace material (Series I/II territory), tracked separately because it's tied to an external, already-in-progress transcription project for Berith Press rather than fresh reading.

**Confirmed titles** (two distinct works — verify which one, or both, is the current Berith Press transcription target):

- **The Ark of the Covenant Opened: or, A Treatise of the Covenant of Redemption Between God and Christ, as the Foundation of the Covenant of Grace** (1677) — published in Gillespie's lifetime (d. 1675, so posthumous by two years); on the covenant of redemption (the intra-Trinitarian pact) underlying the covenant of grace.
- **The Ark of the Testament Opened: or, The Secret of the Lord's Covenant Unsealed, in a Treatise of the Covenant of Grace** (1681) — later, also posthumous, with a preface by John Owen; on the covenant of grace itself. This is the one featured as a series on pro-rege.com ("Ark of Testament Opened").

Already noted in the project's founding conversation and `CLAUDE.md`'s bibliography material: "companion piece on the covenant of redemption feeding into the covenant of grace... useful for seeing how the eternal covenant transaction between Father and Son underlies and guarantees the unity across administrations."
